# Hyrule Castle East Wing Boomerang Puzzle Chest

In the **East Wing** of Hyrule Castle.

Solve the **Boomerang puzzle** -- aim the **Gale Boomerang** through the pinwheels in the right order to open the door. Hint: the **leaves on the floor** show the sequence the wind should flow.

Requires: **Gale Boomerang**.
