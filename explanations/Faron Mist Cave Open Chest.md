# Faron Mist Cave Open Chest

Inside the small **Faron Mist Cave** — the cave whose mouth opens off the east side of the mist area in North Faron Woods (separate from the long tunnel that runs from South Faron).

Once inside, the chest sits openly in the middle of the room — no puzzle, just walk up and open it.

Bring the **Lantern** to clear the mist on the way in.
