# Snowpeak Ruins Broken Floor Chest

In the **caged Freezard room** of Snowpeak Ruins.

The floor has a **cracked section** -- use **Bombs** or the **Ball and Chain** to break it; the chest is revealed below (or reached by dropping through).

Requires: **Bombs** or **Ball and Chain**.
