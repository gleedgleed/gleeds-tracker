# Lake Hylia Bridge Cliff Chest

**North of the Bridge of Hylia**, up on the cliff face.

Use **Bomb Arrows** to blast the rocks off the cliff ledges — that uncovers the **Clawshot targets** you can chain to reach the chest.

Requires: **Bow + Bombs** (Bomb Arrows), **Clawshot**.
