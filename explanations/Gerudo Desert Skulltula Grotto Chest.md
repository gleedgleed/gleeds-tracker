# Gerudo Desert Skulltula Grotto Chest

Inside the **Skulltula Grotto** south of the cannon drop point (wolf-dig grotto).

**Kill every Skulltula** to spawn the chest.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
