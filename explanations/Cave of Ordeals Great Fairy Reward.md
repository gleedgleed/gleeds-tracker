# Cave of Ordeals Great Fairy Reward

On **Floor 50** of the **Cave of Ordeals** (the bottom floor).

Survive every floor of the cave, then **talk to the Great Fairy** at the bottom -- she gives the item.

Requires: clear all 50 floors of the Cave of Ordeals.
