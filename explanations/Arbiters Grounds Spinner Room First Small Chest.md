# Arbiters Grounds Spinner Room First Small Chest

In the **Spinner-rail room** of Arbiter's Grounds (the Stallord pre-fight area with the sand pit).

A small chest on a ledge in the **front** of the room. Mount the **Spinner** and ride to reach.

Requires: **Spinner**.
