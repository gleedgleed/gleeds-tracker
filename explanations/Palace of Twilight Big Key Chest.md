# Palace of Twilight Big Key Chest

In Palace of Twilight. The chest is gated behind a **Wall of Darkness**.

**Use the Light Sword** (Master Sword infused with Sol energy) to dispel the darkness wall, then **Double Clawshot** across to reach the chest.

Requires: **Light-imbued Master Sword**, **Double Clawshot**.
