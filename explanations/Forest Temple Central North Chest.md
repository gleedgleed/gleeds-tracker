# Forest Temple Central North Chest

In the **Lobby** of the Forest Temple, sitting at the top of the **raised north staircase**.

**Light all the torches** in the lobby with the Lantern — that raises the staircase. Climb up and open the chest.

Requires: **Lantern**.
