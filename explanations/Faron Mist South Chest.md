# Faron Mist South Chest

In the poisonous mist area of North Faron Woods, near the locked gate that leads on into North Faron Woods proper. Larger chest guarded by a **Deku Baba** — chop it down, then open.

Bring the **Lantern** to keep the mist back (or use **wolf form**, which is immune).
