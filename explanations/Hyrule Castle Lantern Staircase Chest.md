# Hyrule Castle Lantern Staircase Chest

In the **room with the raisable staircase** (lit-torch-controlled) in Hyrule Castle.

**Defeat the Darknut**, then **light the torches with the Lantern** to raise the staircase, and climb to the chest.

Requires: **Lantern**, sword combat for the Darknut.
