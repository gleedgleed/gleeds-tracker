# Forest Temple West Tile Worm Chest Behind Stairs

In the **West Tile Worm Room** of the Forest Temple. The chest is hidden behind a set of stairs guarded by lit torches.

**Throw the Gale Boomerang** through the lit torches to blow them out — that drops the staircase / opens access to the chest area.

Requires: **Gale Boomerang**.
