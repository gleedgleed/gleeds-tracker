# Gerudo Desert Rock Grotto Lantern Chest

Inside the **Rock Grotto** below the lone Poe ledge in northern Gerudo Desert.

**Destroy the rocks** inside, then **light the torches with the Lantern** to reveal the chest.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal) to enter, **Lantern**, bombs/ball-and-chain to break rocks.
