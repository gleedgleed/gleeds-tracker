# Lake Hylia Bridge Cliff Poe

On the cliff **south of the Bridge of Hylia**.

**Bomb Arrow** the rocks to reveal **Clawshot targets**, then chain up the ledges. The Poe is at the top.

Requires: **Bow + Bombs** (Bomb Arrows), **Clawshot**, **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
