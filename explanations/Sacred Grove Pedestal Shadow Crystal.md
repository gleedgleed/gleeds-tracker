# Sacred Grove Pedestal Shadow Crystal

At the **Master Sword pedestal** at the heart of Sacred Grove.

Approach the pedestal as **Wolf Link** and press **A** — the sword's holy power extracts the Shadow Crystal from your back instead of being drawn.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (or, equivalently, no permanent wolf state — this is the *gift* of the Shadow Crystal that lets you transform at will).
