# Goron Mines Dangoro Chest

The chest that appears after defeating **Dangoro**, the Goron Mines midboss (the armored Goron who tries to roll Link off the platform into lava).

Wear **Iron Boots** during the fight so he can't push you off, then sword him down. The chest spawns in the arena.

Requires: **Iron Boots**.
