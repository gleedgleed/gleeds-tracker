# Death Mountain Volcano Pipe Ledge Rock Rupee

Under the lowest pipe is a ledge with a rock on it. Use a Goron to get on the ledge to get the check.

Requires: Can Defeat Goron or Can Clear Goron Mines, Clear Eldin Twilight.
