# Forest Temple Diababa

Defeat **Diababa**, the Forest Temple boss (giant triple-headed Deku Baba in the poisonous pool).

Use the **Gale Boomerang** to grab a **Bombling** from the side platform and fling it into the central head's mouth, then sword the stunned heads. After two rounds the central head emerges — boomerang another bombling into its mouth and finish it.

Check fires when the boss dies.
