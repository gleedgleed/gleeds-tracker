# Hyrule Field Amphitheater Poe

On the **south side of the West Hyrule Field Amphitheater** (the castle-ruins area overlooking the Bridge of Hylia).

Approach as **Wolf Link** at night, Senses up, fight.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
