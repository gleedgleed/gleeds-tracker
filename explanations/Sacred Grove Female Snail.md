# Sacred Grove Female Snail

Inside Sacred Grove **in the past** — only reachable after stepping through the **Door of Time** (Grove 2, using the Dominion Rod-restored Owl Statues).

She's on the wall on the **right side of the stairs** leading up to the Temple of Time.

Requires: Grove 2 access (Dominion Rod, Master Sword pulled, howling-stone puzzle solved).
