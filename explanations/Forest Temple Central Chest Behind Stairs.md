# Forest Temple Central Chest Behind Stairs

In the **Lobby** (central main room) of the Forest Temple, hidden behind a **boulder** under the north door's staircase.

Pick up a **Bombling** (Bombchu-plant) and lob it at the boulder to blow it open, then access the chest.
