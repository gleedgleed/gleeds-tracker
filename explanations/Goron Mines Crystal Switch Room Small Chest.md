# Goron Mines Crystal Switch Room Small Chest

In the **water-filled room with the crystal switch** in Goron Mines.

The small chest sits on a **ledge near the crystal switch**. Use **Iron Boots** to walk underwater across the floor to reach the ledge.

Requires: **Iron Boots**.
