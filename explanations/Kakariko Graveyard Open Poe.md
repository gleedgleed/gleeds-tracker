# Kakariko Graveyard Open Poe

A **Poe floating openly** in the Kakariko Graveyard at night — no hidden trick, just walk in and you'll see its lantern.

Approach as **Wolf Link** and fight it.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
