# Palace of Twilight Collect Both Sols

Carry **both Sols** (the floating orbs of light from the East and West wings) back to the **entrance / lobby** of Palace of Twilight.

When you've delivered the second Sol, the check fires.

Requires: clear both wing-end Sol rooms.
