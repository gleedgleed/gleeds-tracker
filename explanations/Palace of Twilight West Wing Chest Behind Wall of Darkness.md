# Palace of Twilight West Wing Chest Behind Wall of Darkness

In the **West Wing** of Palace of Twilight.

A **Wall of Darkness** blocks the chest -- **carry a Sol** or use the **Light Sword** to dispel it, then **Clawshot** to reach.

Requires: **Sol** or **Light-imbued Master Sword**, **Clawshot**.
