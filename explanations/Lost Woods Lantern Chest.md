# Lost Woods Lantern Chest

In the **Lost Woods** (the maze section before Sacred Grove proper). Navigate the woods following Skull Kid's horn cues — at one point you'll come to a pair of **unlit torches**.

**Light both torches** with the Lantern; the chest spawns.

Requires: **Lantern**.
