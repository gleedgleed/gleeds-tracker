# Gerudo Desert Owl Statue Chest

In the **south-central area of Gerudo Desert**.

**Powered Dominion Rod** the Owl Statue and walk it across the sand as stepping stones to reach the chest's ledge.

Requires: **Powered Dominion Rod**.
