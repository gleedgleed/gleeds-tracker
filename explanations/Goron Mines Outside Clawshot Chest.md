# Goron Mines Outside Clawshot Chest

In the **outside area** of Goron Mines (open canyon section).

On the **north side** of the room there are **hanging ropes** — Clawshot from one to the next to swing across, landing on the ledge with the chest.

Requires: **Clawshot**.
