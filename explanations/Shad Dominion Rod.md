# Shad Dominion Rod

Given by **Shad** in Castle Town's Telma's Bar (the Resistance hangout).

**Show him the Ancient Sky Book** (Skybook From Impaz). He'll demonstrate the lost language and hand over the **Dominion Rod**.

Requires: **Skybook From Impaz**.
