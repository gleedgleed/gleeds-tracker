# Arbiters Grounds Entrance Chest

In the **Entrance Room** of Arbiter's Grounds.

**Pull the chain** (Wolf Link) to open the gate, then **break the wooden gate** beyond it to reach the chest.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal) for the chain, plus a way to break wood (sword bash or similar).
