# Kakariko Gorge Spire Heart Piece

On top of the **rock spire to the north of the bridge** in Kakariko Gorge — the same tall spire as the Double Clawshot puzzle.

Reach the top of the spire (Double Clawshot up the targets) and the heart piece sits there.

Requires: **Double Clawshot** (or sufficient Bomb Arrows + climb to reach the freestanding pickup at the apex).
