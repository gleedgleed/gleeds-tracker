# Bridge of Eldin Owl Statue Chest

On the **Bridge of Eldin**. There's an **Owl Statue at the north end** of the bridge.

Use the **Powered Dominion Rod** to lift it, carry it along the bridge to the **south side**, and drop it into the empty socket **across from the Bulblin Archer**. Use the statue as a stepping stone to reach a small entry with a **ladder** — climb to the chest at the top.

Requires: **Powered Dominion Rod**.
