# Lanayru Field Behind Gate Underwater Chest

In Lanayru Field, **south of the stone bridge**, underwater behind a **wall of wooden gates**.

Break the gates (Gale Boomerang, bombs, etc.), then equip **Iron Boots** to sink and open the chest.

Requires: **Iron Boots**, plus a way to break the gates (Gale Boomerang works well).
