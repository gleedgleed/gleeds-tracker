# Zoras Domain Waterfall Poe

On a high ledge in **Zora's Domain**, near the **upper waterfall**.

Two routes:
- As **Wolf Link**, **Midna-jump** up from the **Snowpeak entrance area** (the lower river path).
- Or **drop down from the top** of the Domain (cliff path) onto the Poe's ledge.

Either way, fight as Wolf.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
