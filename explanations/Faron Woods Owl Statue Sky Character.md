# Faron Woods Owl Statue Sky Character

In South Faron Woods, **just north of Coro's hut** — the small Owl Statue alcove behind the cracked wall (bomb the wall to open it).

Use the **Powered Dominion Rod** to lift the owl statue and shove it off its pedestal. Stand on the now-exposed circular socket as Wolf Link / step on it; the Sky Character is engraved underneath and gets added to the Sky Book.

Requires: **Powered Dominion Rod** (post-Hidden-Village), **Bombs** (to break the wall), **Wolf Form**.
