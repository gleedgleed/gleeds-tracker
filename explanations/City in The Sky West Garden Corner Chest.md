# City in The Sky West Garden Corner Chest

In the **West Wing upper open area** (the garden with Peahats) of City in The Sky.

Chest is in the **corner of one of the Peahat sections** -- Clawshot a Peahat to reach.

Requires: **Clawshot** (or Double Clawshot for the Peahat chain).
