# Palace of Twilight East Wing First Room East Alcove Chest

In the **East Wing first room** of Palace of Twilight.

The **east alcove platform** is inactive -- use a **Sol** (carry the orb in) or the **Light Sword** to activate it. Then step on and ride to the chest.

Requires: **Sol** in possession, or **Light-imbued Master Sword**.
