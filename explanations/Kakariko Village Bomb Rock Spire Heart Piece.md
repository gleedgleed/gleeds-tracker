# Kakariko Village Bomb Rock Spire Heart Piece

Near the **south entrance to Kakariko Village**, sitting on top of a tall **rock spire**.

You can't climb the spire — **shoot bomb arrows** at the base / the supports to blow it down (or otherwise break it open) and the heart piece becomes reachable.

Requires: **Bow + Bombs** (Bomb Arrows).
