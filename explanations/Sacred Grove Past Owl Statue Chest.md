# Sacred Grove Past Owl Statue Chest

In the **past** version of Sacred Grove, after stepping through the **Door of Time** (Grove 2).

Use the **Dominion Rod** on the Owl Statue near the stairway — moving it clears the path to the chest. Walk over and open.

Requires: **Dominion Rod**, Grove 2 access.
