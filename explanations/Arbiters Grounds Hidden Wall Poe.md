# Arbiters Grounds Hidden Wall Poe

In the **hallway with the Stalchildren and Redeads** in Arbiter's Grounds.

**Pull the chain buried in the sand** (Wolf-dig at the patch first) to expose / open the hidden wall and reveal the Poe.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
