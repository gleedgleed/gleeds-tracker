# Goron Mines Outside Underwater Chest

In the **outside area** of Goron Mines, **underwater**.

A **wooden gate** blocks the chest. Break the gate (boomerang, bombs, or other ranged) then equip **Iron Boots** and sink to reach it.

Requires: **Iron Boots**, plus a way to break the wooden gate (e.g. Gale Boomerang).
