# Faron Mist Cave Lantern Chest

Inside the small **Faron Mist Cave** (mouth on the east side of the mist area in North Faron Woods).

Light **both** of the unlit torches in the cave with your **Lantern**. A chest then appears on the ledge against the back wall.

(Bring the Lantern for the mist outside, too.)
