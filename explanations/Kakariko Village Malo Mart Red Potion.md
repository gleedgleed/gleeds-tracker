# Kakariko Village Malo Mart Red Potion

For sale at **Malo Mart** in Kakariko Village.

Unlocked **after Eldin Twilight is cleared** *and* the **Hylian Shield** has been purchased from Malo (Red Potion only shows up on the shelf after the shield is gone).
