# Wrestling With Bo

Inside **Mayor Bo's house** in Ordon Village. Talk to Bo and accept the sumo wrestling minigame.

You must **defeat Bo twice** — the chest appears after the second win. (He puts on the Iron Boots for the rematch, so equipping them yourself or using grapple/push tactics is the trick.)
