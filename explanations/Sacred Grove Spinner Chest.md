# Sacred Grove Spinner Chest

In one of the **Skull Kid arenas** in Sacred Grove — the one fitted with **Spinner tracks** along the walls.

Equip the **Spinner**, mount the tracks, and ride to the elevated ledge where the chest sits.

Requires: **Spinner**.
