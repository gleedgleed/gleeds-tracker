# Lake Lantern Cave Thirteenth Chest

In the **Lake Lantern Cave** (the long cave at the south end of Lake Hylia), one of the last rooms before the end.

Use the **Lantern** to see your way through the dark cave; **smash any boulders** that block the path. The chest sits on the route.

Requires: **Lantern** (highly recommended; bombs/Ball-and-Chain to clear boulders).
