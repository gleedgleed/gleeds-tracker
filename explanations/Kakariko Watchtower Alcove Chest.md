# Kakariko Watchtower Alcove Chest

On a high **ledge near the watchtower** at the top of Kakariko Village — too far to jump.

**Free the trapped Cuccos** near the south entrance (smash the rocks blocking them), pick one up and carry it all the way up the village to the watchtower, then **glide off the watchtower top** to drift onto the alcove ledge with the chest.

Requires: ability to break the rocks (bombs / Ball and Chain / etc.) freeing the Cuccos.
