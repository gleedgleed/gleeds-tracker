# Goron Mines Fyrus

Defeat **Fyrus**, the Goron Mines boss (the flaming, chained Goron in the lava arena).

**Shoot the gem on his forehead with the Bow** to stun him, then **grab his trailing chain** and pull to topple him. Sword the gem while he's down. Repeat until he falls.

Check fires when the boss dies.

Requires: **Hero's Bow**.
