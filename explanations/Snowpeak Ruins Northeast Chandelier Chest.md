# Snowpeak Ruins Northeast Chandelier Chest

On the **upper level of the northeastern-most room** of Snowpeak Ruins.

**Ball-and-Chain the chandelier** to swing it, then ride it (or land on the ledge it exposes) to reach the chest.

Requires: **Ball and Chain**.
