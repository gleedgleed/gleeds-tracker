# Kakariko Graveyard Grave Poe

In the **Kakariko Graveyard**. **Push open one of the graves** (the gravestones slide aside) to release a hidden Poe.

Approach as **Wolf Link**, then fight it after it pops out.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
