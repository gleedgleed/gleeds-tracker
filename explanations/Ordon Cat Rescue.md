# Ordon Cat Rescue

The prologue cat-rescue quest. Borrow the **Fishing Rod** from Sera, go to the pond/dock, and **catch two fish** — the cat will follow you, and you carry it back to Sera's shop.

Reward is given by Sera once the cat is delivered. The check fires when she hands it over.
