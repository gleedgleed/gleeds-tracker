# West Hyrule Field Golden Wolf

On a **ledge near the west entrance to Castle Town** (West Hyrule Field side).

Only spawns **after you've howled at the Upper Zora's River howling stone**.

Approach in **Wolf Form** to learn the Hidden Skill.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), Upper Zora's River howling stone played.
