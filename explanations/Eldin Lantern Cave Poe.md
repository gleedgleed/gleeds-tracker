# Eldin Lantern Cave Poe

In the **Lantern Cave** (Kakariko Gorge entrance), at one of the cave's **dead-end branches**.

Wander the dead ends in **Wolf Form** with Senses up — the Poe is floating in one of them.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), **Lantern** (highly recommended for navigation).
