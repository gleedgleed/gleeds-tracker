# Ordon Spring Golden Wolf

The **Golden Wolf** appears at **Ordon Spring**, near the **crawlspace** (the dig-hole that leads under the spring's back wall).

He only spawns **after you've howled at the Death Mountain howling stone** (the one on the path up Death Mountain that teaches the first tune). Once that's done, return to Ordon Spring in Wolf form and approach him to learn the Hidden Skill.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), Death Mountain howling stone played.
