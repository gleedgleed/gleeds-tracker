# Eldin Stockcave Upper Chest

Inside the **Goron Stockcave** (cave entered from a ledge off Eldin Bridge / Eldin Field).

Drop down **two levels** into the cave — a small chest sits there, guarded by a **Dodongo**. Kill the Dodongo, open the chest.
