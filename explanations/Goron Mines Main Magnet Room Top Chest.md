# Goron Mines Main Magnet Room Top Chest

In the **big multi-level magnet room** in Goron Mines.

A chest sits at the **very top of the room**. Reachable **only after defeating Dangoro** (the magnetic-floor mechanic post-midboss is what gets you up there).

Requires: **Iron Boots**, Dangoro defeated.
