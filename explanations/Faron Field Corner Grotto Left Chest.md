# Faron Field Corner Grotto Left Chest

Inside the **Corner Grotto** in the northeast corner of Faron Field — the grass-circle hole that only **Wolf Link** can dig open. (Grotto is filled with Keese and Rats.)

Small chest hidden in the **tall grass on the left side** of the room. Cut the grass to reveal it.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
