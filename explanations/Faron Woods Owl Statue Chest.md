# Faron Woods Owl Statue Chest

A chest on a high ledge in the Faron Woods mist area, reached only after you've solved the **South Faron Owl Statue** puzzle (see "Faron Woods Owl Statue Sky Character").

After lifting the owl statue with the **Powered Dominion Rod** and dropping it into its socket, the statue becomes a stepping-stone. Use **Midna's wolf-form jump** to hop from the statue up onto the high ledge where the chest sits.

Requires: **Powered Dominion Rod**, **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), and Faron Twilight cleared.
