# Lake Hylia Water Toadpoli Grotto Chest

In the **grotto outside the Lake Lantern Cave** at Lake Hylia (wolf-dig grotto).

**Kill every Water Toadpoli** inside; the chest appears.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
