# Lakebed Temple Deku Toad Chest

The chest that appears in the **Deku Toad midboss arena** after defeating the **Deku Toad** (the giant toad spitting Toadpolis).

Wear **Iron Boots** to stay grounded under the toad's stomps, sword its tongue when it lashes, then sword its underside.

Requires: **Iron Boots**.
