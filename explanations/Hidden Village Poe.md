# Hidden Village Poe

On a **ledge near the entrance of the Hidden Village**.

**Defeat the Bulblins** attacking the village, then **show Ilia the Charm** -- only after both conditions does the Poe spawn. Fight as Wolf Link.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), Hidden Village Bulblin attack cleared, **Ilia's Charm** shown to Ilia.
