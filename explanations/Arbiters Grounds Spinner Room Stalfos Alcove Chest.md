# Arbiters Grounds Spinner Room Stalfos Alcove Chest

In the **Spinner-rail room** of Arbiter's Grounds.

A chest in an **alcove guarded by Stalfos**. Reach it by riding one of the **Spinner rails** off into the alcove.

Requires: **Spinner**.
