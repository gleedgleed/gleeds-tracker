# Lanayru Field Spinner Track Chest

On a **Spinner track running along the cliff wall on the west side of Lanayru Field**.

**Bomb the rocks** to expose the track entry, then mount the **Spinner** and ride along the wall to reach the chest.

Requires: **Bombs** (to clear rocks), **Spinner**.
