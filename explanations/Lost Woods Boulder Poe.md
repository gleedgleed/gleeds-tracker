# Lost Woods Boulder Poe

In the **first Skull Kid arena** in the Lost Woods — the open clearing where you initially fight Skull Kid as Wolf Link.

A **boulder** in this arena hides the Poe. **Destroy the rock** (bombs or Ball and Chain) to release it, then fight it as Wolf.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), bombs / Ball and Chain to break the rock.
