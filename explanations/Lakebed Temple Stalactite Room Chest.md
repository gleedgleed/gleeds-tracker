# Lakebed Temple Stalactite Room Chest

In the **room north of the Lobby** of Lakebed Temple.

**Destroy the hanging stalactites** to clear the way to the chest (Clawshot Bomb Plants, or Bomb Arrows).

Requires: way to break stalactites.
