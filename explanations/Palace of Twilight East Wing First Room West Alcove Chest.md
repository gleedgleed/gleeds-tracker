# Palace of Twilight East Wing First Room West Alcove Chest

In the **East Wing first room** of Palace of Twilight.

The **west alcove platform** is inactive -- use a **Sol** or the **Light Sword** to activate it, then ride to the chest.

Requires: **Sol** or **Light-imbued Master Sword**.
