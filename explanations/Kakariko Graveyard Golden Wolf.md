# Kakariko Graveyard Golden Wolf

In the **Kakariko Graveyard** (behind the village's sanctuary). Stands in front of the **stone slab that hides the Zora Tomb crawlspace**.

Approach in **Wolf Form** to enter the Hero's Shade vision and learn the Hidden Skill.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
