# City in The Sky East Wing After Dinalfos Alcove Chest

In the **upper room of the East Wing with the Ooccoo** in City in The Sky.

**Clawshot the switch** to open an **alcove**, then **grab an Ooccoo (chicken-like flying creature)** and glide over to the newly-opened alcove for the chest.

Requires: **Clawshot**.
