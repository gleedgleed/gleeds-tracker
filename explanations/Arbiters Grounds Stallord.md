# Arbiters Grounds Stallord

Defeat **Stallord**, the Arbiter's Grounds boss (giant Stalfos skeleton rising out of the sand).

**Spinner** into the spine to topple Stallord. After the head detaches and starts flying, ride the **Spinner up the central pillar**, jump off when Stallord's head charges your rail, and slash the head mid-air.

Requires: **Spinner**.
