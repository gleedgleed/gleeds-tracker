# North Faron Woods Deku Baba Chest

Small chest in **North Faron Woods** — the open area between the mist gate and the bridge leading to the Forest Temple entrance. It sits off to the side of the main path, guarded by a **Deku Baba**.

Chop the Deku Baba down with your sword, then open the chest. Nothing else required.
