# Ordon Shield

Hanging on the wall **inside Jaggle and Pergie's house** (Talo and Malo's parents — the house with the high attic in Ordon Village).

You can **knock it down as Wolf Link before Faron Twilight is cleared** — sneak in as wolf and jump at the shield to drop it.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
