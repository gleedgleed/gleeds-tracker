# Auru Gift To Fyer

After **Lanayru Twilight is cleared**, talk to **Auru** — he's on top of the **wooden tower at Lake Hylia** (next to Fyer's cannon).

He hands you a gift to deliver to Fyer — that's the check.

Requires: Lanayru Twilight cleared.
