# Palace of Twilight Zant

Defeat **Zant**, the Palace of Twilight boss.

Multi-phase fight: Zant teleports through five sub-arenas (Forest, Lakebed, Snowpeak, Twilight, Hyrule Field) cycling familiar mechanics from each dungeon. Use the appropriate item in each phase (Gale Boomerang, Iron Boots, Ball and Chain, swords-and-Sols, etc.). Final phase is a sword duel.

Requires: Boss progression items as appropriate (Boomerang, Iron Boots, Ball and Chain, Master Sword, etc.).
