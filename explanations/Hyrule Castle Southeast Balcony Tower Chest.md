# Hyrule Castle Southeast Balcony Tower Chest

In the **southeast balcony tower** of Hyrule Castle.

**Defeat the Aeralfos** (winged Lizalfos) -- the door to the chest opens after.

Requires: **Clawshot** (to pull down the Aeralfos's shield).
