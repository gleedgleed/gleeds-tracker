# Kakariko Gorge Owl Statue Chest

In the **northern part of Kakariko Gorge** (above the entrance to Kakariko Village).

Use the **Powered Dominion Rod** on the Owl Statue and **move it into the gaps** in the ground — you walk it across as stepping stones to reach the chest's ledge.

Requires: **Powered Dominion Rod**.
