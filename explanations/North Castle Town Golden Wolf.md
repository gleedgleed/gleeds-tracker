# North Castle Town Golden Wolf

The Golden Wolf in front of the **entrance to Hyrule Castle** (north end of Castle Town).

Only spawns **after you've howled at the Hidden Village howling stone**.

Approach in **Wolf Form** to learn the Hidden Skill.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), Hidden Village howling stone played.
