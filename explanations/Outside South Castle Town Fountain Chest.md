# Outside South Castle Town Fountain Chest

At the **fountain south of Castle Town**.

**Clawshot the right-side pillar** above the fountain, then mount the **Spinner** and ride the track — jump off the track onto the platform with the chest.

Requires: **Clawshot**, **Spinner**.
