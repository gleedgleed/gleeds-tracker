# Snowpeak Ruins Lobby Chandelier Chest

In the **Lobby** of Snowpeak Ruins.

Use the **Ball and Chain** to **knock the chandeliers off-balance** and ride one over to the chest (or have it drop nearby).

Requires: **Ball and Chain**.
