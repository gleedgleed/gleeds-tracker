# Temple of Time Armos Antechamber East Chest

In the **Armos Antechamber** of Temple of Time (the room with the two Armos guarding the path).

**Defeat the Armos** -- the east chest is revealed.

Requires: ability to kill Armos (sword + dominion rod activation; or simply sword the right body part).
