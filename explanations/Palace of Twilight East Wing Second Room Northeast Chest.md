# Palace of Twilight East Wing Second Room Northeast Chest

In the **East Wing second room** of Palace of Twilight.

**Defeat all the Shadow Beasts** (the lurking-portal kind), then **Double Clawshot** across to the chest in the northeast.

Requires: **Double Clawshot**.
