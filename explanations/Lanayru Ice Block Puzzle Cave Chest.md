# Lanayru Ice Block Puzzle Cave Chest

Inside the **Ice Block Puzzle Cave** in Lanayru Field.

The entrance is blocked by a giant ice block — **smash it with the Ball and Chain**. Inside, solve the **ice-pushing puzzle** to reach the chest at the back.

Requires: **Ball and Chain**.
