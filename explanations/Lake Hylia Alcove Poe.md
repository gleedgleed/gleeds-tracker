# Lake Hylia Alcove Poe

Near the **Lake Hylia warp portal**, head **south** — there's a small **alcove** in the cliff wall.

The Poe hides inside. Fight as **Wolf Link**.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
