# Lost Woods Waterfall Poe

On a ledge inside an **alcove behind a waterfall** in the Lost Woods — swim into the waterfall to reach the alcove.

Only reachable **during or after Grove 2** (the second Sacred Grove visit, after the Master Sword chamber has reopened with Dominion-Rod access).

Requires: **Wolf Form** to fight the Poe, Grove 2 access (Dominion Rod / appropriate progression).
