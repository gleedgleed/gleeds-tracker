# Coro Bottle

South Faron Woods, at Coro's spot (the open clearing in the south of Faron Woods, near the path from Ordon — Coro is the green-haired guy who hands out the **Lantern**).

The check is the **bottle of Lantern Oil** he sells. Talk to him and buy it — normally 100 rupees.
