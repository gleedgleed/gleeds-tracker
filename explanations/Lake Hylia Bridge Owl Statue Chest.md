# Lake Hylia Bridge Owl Statue Chest

At the **Owl Statue south of the Bridge of Hylia**.

**Powered Dominion Rod** to move the statue as a stepping stone, then jump from it onto the chest's ledge.

Requires: **Powered Dominion Rod**.
