# Goron Springwater Rush

The **springwater delivery minigame**. Available **after donating 1,000 rupees to Malo Mart** in Kakariko Village to rebuild the Castle Town bridge.

Once the bridge is fixed, talk to **Gor Liggs** (he sets up near Eldin Bridge) to start the minigame: carry a **barrel of hot springwater** from the spring all the way to the tired Goron before it cools.

Requires: Bridge donations complete (1,000 rupees to Malo Mart).
