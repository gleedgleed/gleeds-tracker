# Faron Mist Poe

On top of the big tree stump at the very center of the poisonous mist area in Faron Woods.

You have to be in **wolf form** — Midna-jump (Z-target lock-on then hop) across the smaller stumps to reach the center stump, then fight the Poe with the senses-vision attack.
