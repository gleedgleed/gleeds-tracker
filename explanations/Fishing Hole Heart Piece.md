# Fishing Hole Heart Piece

In **Hena's Fishing Hole**, on the **small cliff/island in the middle of the pond**.

Two ways to reach it:
- **Rent the boat** from Hena and use the **Fishing Rod** to snag the heart piece from the water.
- Or swim out and **Clawshot** to the cliff while swimming.

Requires: **Fishing Rod** (boat method) or **Clawshot** (swim method).
