# Iza Raging Rapids Minigame

The **canoe minigame** at **Iza's Shop** in Upper Zora's River (only available after the **Iza Helping Hand** quest has been completed and the river is open).

Pay Iza, take the canoe, and shoot pots / hit targets to **score 25 points or more** in a run.

Requires: **Bow**, Iza Helping Hand completed.
