# Eldin Lantern Cave First Chest

Inside the **Lantern Cave** (the big multi-fork cave entered from Kakariko Gorge).

From the entrance, take **left at the first fork, then right** at the next. You'll find a **Skulltula guarding** the chest — kill it, open the chest.

Requires: **Lantern** (the cave is pitch-dark — much harder to navigate without it).
