# Hyrule Castle Ganondorf

Defeat **Ganondorf**, the final boss, at the top of Hyrule Castle (and the field after).

Multi-phase fight: Beast Ganon (Bow to the gem on his forehead, then sword the wound while wolf-grappling); Ganondorf Horseback (Light Arrows + chasing on Epona while Zelda fires); Sword Ganondorf (parry duel, finish with Ending Blow).

Requires: **Bow**, **Master Sword**, **Epona** (with Zelda riding).
