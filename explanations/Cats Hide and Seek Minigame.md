# Cats Hide and Seek Minigame

In the **Hidden Village**. After **showing Ilia's Charm to Ilia** (in Kakariko sanctuary), return to the Hidden Village as **Wolf Link** and talk to the **Cucco Leader** (the head cat / cucco running things).

You play hide-and-seek: find each cat hidden around the village.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), Ilia's Charm shown to Ilia.
