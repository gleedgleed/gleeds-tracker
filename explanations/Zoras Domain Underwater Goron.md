# Zoras Domain Underwater Goron

In the **underwater section of Zora's Domain** — there's a Goron frozen inside a **meteor at the bottom**.

**Throw a Water Bomb** at the meteor to crack it open. Equip **Iron Boots** to sink, then talk to the freed Goron — he hands over the item.

Requires: **Water Bombs**, **Iron Boots**.
