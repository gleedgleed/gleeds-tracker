# City in The Sky Central Outside Poe Island Chest

In the **outside room above the central fan** in City in The Sky.

Walk the tightropes as **Wolf Link** to reach the **small island with a Poe** -- the chest is also on this island.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
