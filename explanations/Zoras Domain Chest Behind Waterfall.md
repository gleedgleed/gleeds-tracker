# Zoras Domain Chest Behind Waterfall

In **Zora's Domain**, on a ledge behind one of the waterfalls.

As **Wolf Link**, **Midna-jump** across the small stone ledges along the cliff face that lead to the chest.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
