# Temple of Time Poe Above Scales

Near the **door to the Big Key room** in Temple of Time -- there's a **ledge above** with the Poe on it.

Fight as **Wolf Link**.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), means to reach the upper ledge (Spinner / Clawshot).
