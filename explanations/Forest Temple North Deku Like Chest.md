# Forest Temple North Deku Like Chest

In the room **west of the pre-boss room** in the Forest Temple. A **Deku Like** (the green floor-mouth that swallows Link) blocks the chest.

Use the **Gale Boomerang to grab a Bombling** from across the room and lob it into the Deku Like's mouth. Boom — chest accessible.

Requires: **Gale Boomerang**.
