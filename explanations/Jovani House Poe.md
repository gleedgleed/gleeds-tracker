# Jovani House Poe

**Outside Jovani's house** in west Castle Town — there's a **dig patch** in the dirt by his door.

Switch to **Wolf Link**, dig at the patch — you enter a small underground chamber with the Poe inside. Fight it.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
