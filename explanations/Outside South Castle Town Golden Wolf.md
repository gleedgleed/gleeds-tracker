# Outside South Castle Town Golden Wolf

The Golden Wolf at the **base of the stairs south of Castle Town**, just outside the south gate.

Only spawns **after you've howled at the North Faron Woods howling stone** (the stone on the path to the Forest Temple).

Approach in **Wolf Form** to learn the Hidden Skill.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), North Faron Woods howling stone played.
