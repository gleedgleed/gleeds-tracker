# Ordon Sword

Sitting on the **couch in Rusl and Uli's house** in Ordon Village.

You can grab it as **Wolf Link before Faron Twilight is cleared** — sneak in as wolf and interact with the sword on the couch.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
