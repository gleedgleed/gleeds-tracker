# Ilias Charm

In the **Hidden Village**. **Defeat all the Bulblins** attacking the village (snipe them off rooftops with the Bow), then talk to **Impaz** -- she hands over the charm.

Requires: **Bow** (recommended for the Bulblin sniping), Hidden Village reached.
