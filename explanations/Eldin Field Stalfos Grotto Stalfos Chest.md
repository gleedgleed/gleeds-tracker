# Eldin Field Stalfos Grotto Stalfos Chest

Inside the **Stalfos Grotto** north of Hidden Village.

**Defeat the Stalfos** to make the chest appear.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal) to enter the grotto.
