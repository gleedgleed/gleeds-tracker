# Sacred Grove Master Sword Poe

In the **Master Sword pedestal chamber** in Sacred Grove (the open arena with the pedestal at center).

Only reachable **after Grove 2** — once the pedestal area has been "re-opened" via the Dominion Rod sequence.

Approach as **Wolf Link**, use Senses to spot it, then fight.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), Grove 2 progression.
