# Hyrule Castle Graveyard Owl Statue Chest

In Hyrule Castle's **Graveyard**.

Multi-step:
1. **Bomb the boulder** to open the spirit-graves room.
2. **Light the torch with the Lantern** to stop the rain.
3. Run to the locked gate's **two torches** and light both **before it rains again**.
4. Pass through the gate, then use the **Powered Dominion Rod** to move the **Owl Statues** into their sockets, forming a bridge to the chest.

Requires: **Bombs**, **Lantern**, **Powered Dominion Rod**.
