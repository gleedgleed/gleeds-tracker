# Snowpeak Cave Ice Lantern Chest

In the **wolf-dig cave** on the way up Snowpeak.

**Break the ice** inside with the **Ball and Chain**, then **light the torches** with the **Lantern** to reveal the chest.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal) to enter, **Ball and Chain**, **Lantern**.
