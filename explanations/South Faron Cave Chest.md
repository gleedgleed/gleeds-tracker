# South Faron Cave Chest

Inside the cave that tunnels from South Faron Woods (behind the locked gate just north of Coro) up to the Mist Area in North Faron.

Once you're in the cave, take the short dead-end side path — the small chest sits at its end.

Reaching the cave requires either the **Coro Gate Key** (small key from Coro) or **wolf form** to dig under the gate.
