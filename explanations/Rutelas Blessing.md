# Rutelas Blessing

In the **Kakariko Graveyard**. Once you have the **Gate Keys**, the spirit of **Queen Rutela** appears.

**Follow her** as she floats through the graveyard — she leads you to the Zora royal tomb at the rear and bestows the blessing (the Zora Armor) on Link.

Requires: **Gate Keys**.
