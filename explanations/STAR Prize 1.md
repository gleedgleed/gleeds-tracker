# STAR Prize 1

The **STAR Game minigame** — talk to Purlo running the **STAR tent** in west Castle Town.

**Collect every star** in the tent within the time limit using the **Clawshot** to win the first prize.

Requires: **Clawshot**, **20 rupee** entry fee.
