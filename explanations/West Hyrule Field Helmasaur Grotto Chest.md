# West Hyrule Field Helmasaur Grotto Chest

Inside the **Helmasaur Grotto** on the **west side of Hyrule Field** (on a ledge — wolf-dig entrance).

**Kill all the Helmasaurs** to spawn the chest.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal) to enter.
