# Hyrule Castle Graveyard Grave Switch Room Back Left Chest

In Hyrule Castle's **Graveyard**, in the **grave-switch room**.

Entry: **bomb the boulder** in front of the spirit-guarded grave to open the door, then enter and grab the **back-left chest**.

Requires: **Bombs**.
