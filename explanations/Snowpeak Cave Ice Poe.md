# Snowpeak Cave Ice Poe

In the **wolf-dig cave** on the way to the mountain top in Snowpeak.

A Poe is **frozen in ice**. Smash the ice with the **Ball and Chain** to free it, then fight as Wolf.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), **Ball and Chain**.
