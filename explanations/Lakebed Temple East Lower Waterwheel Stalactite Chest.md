# Lakebed Temple East Lower Waterwheel Stalactite Chest

In the **lower East Wing** of Lakebed Temple.

**Destroy the hanging stalactite** above the chest (Clawshot a Bomb Plant up, or shoot it down with Bomb Arrows). The chest is exposed underneath.

Requires: a way to break stalactites (Clawshot + Bomb Plants / Bomb Arrows / Ball and Chain).
