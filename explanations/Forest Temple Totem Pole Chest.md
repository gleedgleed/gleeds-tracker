# Forest Temple Totem Pole Chest

In the **West Tile Worm Room** of the Forest Temple. A chest sits on top of the **Totem Pole** (the stacked-monkey-statue column).

**Roll into the Totem Pole** (A-button shoulder ram) to topple it — the chest falls to the floor.
