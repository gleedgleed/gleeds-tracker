# Snowpeak Ruins West Cannon Room Corner Chest

In the **frozen cannon room** in the West Wing of Snowpeak Ruins.

The chest is in the corner, blocked by ice -- use **the cannon** or **Ball and Chain** to break it.

Requires: **Ball and Chain** (or cannon).
