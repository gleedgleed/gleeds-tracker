# Hyrule Castle Graveyard Grave Switch Room Right Chest

In Hyrule Castle's **Graveyard**, in the **grave-switch room**.

**Bomb the spirit-guarded grave's boulder** to open the door, then grab the **right-side chest**.

Requires: **Bombs**.
