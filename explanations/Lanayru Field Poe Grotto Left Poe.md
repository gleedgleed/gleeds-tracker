# Lanayru Field Poe Grotto Left Poe

Inside the **Poe Grotto** on the **west side of Lanayru Field** — wolf-dig grass-circle entrance.

Two Poes are floating inside; this one is the **left** Poe. Fight as Wolf Link.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
