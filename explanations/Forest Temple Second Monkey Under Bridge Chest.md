# Forest Temple Second Monkey Under Bridge Chest

In the room where the **second caged monkey** sits atop the **Totem Pole** (the West Wing room before the boss approach).

Look **under the wooden bridge/ledge** in that room — the chest is tucked underneath, easy to miss if you only look up at the cage.
