# Temple of Time Darknut Chest

The chest in the **Darknut midboss arena** in Temple of Time, revealed after defeating the **Darknut** (heavy armored knight).

Strip his armor with the Back Slice, then sword him down.

Requires: sword combat (Back Slice / Helm Splitter / Mortal Draw helpful).
