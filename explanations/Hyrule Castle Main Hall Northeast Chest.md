# Hyrule Castle Main Hall Northeast Chest

In the **Main Hall** of Hyrule Castle, in the northeast.

**Defeat all enemies** in the room, then **Clawshot the chandelier** to swing across to the chest's perch.

Requires: **Clawshot**.
