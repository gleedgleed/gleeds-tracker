# Eldin Field Bomskit Grotto Lantern Chest

Inside the **Bomskit Grotto** in Eldin Field (the wolf-dig grotto with Bomskits running around).

**Light the torches** with the **Lantern** to spawn the chest.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal) to enter the grotto, **Lantern**.
