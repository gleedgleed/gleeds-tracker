# East Castle Town Bridge Poe

In the small **east-Castle-Town area** that connects to Eldin Field (east side of the Hyrule Castle moat).

The Poe sits on the **bridge** in that area — at night, in **Wolf Form**, use Senses and fight it.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
