# Gerudo Desert Northeast Chest Behind Gates

In the **northeast of Gerudo Desert**, behind **wooden gates** near the Bulblin camp.

Clear the Bulblins around the campfire, then **ram the gates with a Boar** to break them open.

Requires: Boar.
