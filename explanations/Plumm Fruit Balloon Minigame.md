# Plumm Fruit Balloon Minigame

Talk to **Plumm**, the floating bird at Lake Hylia. He runs a **fruit-balloon popping minigame** — you ride a Cucco from Falbi's and pop floating balloons before time runs out.

**Score at least 10,000 points** to win.

Requires: Flight By Fowl access (Cucco for the flight).
