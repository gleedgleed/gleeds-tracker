# Lakebed Temple East Water Supply Clawshot Chest

In the **East Water Supply** room of Lakebed Temple -- the vertical shaft with the water flow.

**Clawshot the targets at the top** of the room to reach the chest's perch.

Requires: **Clawshot**.
