# Snowpeak Ruins Lobby Armor Poe

In the **Lobby** of Snowpeak Ruins. A Poe is trapped inside one of the **suits of armor** lining the walls.

**Ball-and-Chain a suit of armor** to shatter it and release the Poe.

Requires: **Ball and Chain**, **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal) for the Poe fight.
