# Eldin Lantern Cave Lantern Chest

Inside the **Lantern Cave** (Kakariko Gorge entrance).

From the entrance, take **right twice, left once, right again** at the forks. In that room, **light the torches** with the Lantern and the chest appears.

Requires: **Lantern**.
