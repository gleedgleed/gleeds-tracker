# Outside Lanayru Spring Left Statue Chest

On top of the **left statue outside the Lanayru Spring** entrance (Lake Hylia side).

Take a **Cucco from Falbi's Flight By Fowl** house above and glide over to the statue's top to reach the chest.

Requires: Flight By Fowl access.
