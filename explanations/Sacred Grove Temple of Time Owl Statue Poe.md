# Sacred Grove Temple of Time Owl Statue Poe

By the **Owl Statue** near the entrance to the Temple of Time (Grove 2's upper area).

Use the **Dominion Rod** to move the Owl Statue — the Poe is released from underneath. Fight it as **Wolf Link**.

Requires: **Dominion Rod**, **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), Grove 2 access.
