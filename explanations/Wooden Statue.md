# Wooden Statue

Given by **Louise the cat** at her spot south of Castle Town (she sits near the south fountain at night, you talk to her as Wolf Link).

Prerequisites:
1. Collect the **Medicine Scent** from Coro (in Faron Woods, given after delivering Renado's letter etc.).
2. Talk to **Louise** about it.
3. Then defeat all of the **Stallhounds** that spawn at **sundown** in southern Castle Town field.

Once the Stallhounds are cleared, return to Louise for the Wooden Statue.

Requires: **Medicine Scent**, **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal) to talk to Louise.
