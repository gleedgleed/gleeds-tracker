# Temple of Time Poe Behind Gate

In the **room with the central turning platform** in Temple of Time.

Use the **Dominion Rod** to move a **statue onto a floor switch**, opening the gate that hides the Poe.

Requires: **Dominion Rod**, **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
