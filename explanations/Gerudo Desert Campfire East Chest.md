# Gerudo Desert Campfire East Chest

In **Gerudo Desert**, near the **central Bulblin campfire**. A wooden **tower** blocks the chest on the east side.

**Defeat the Bulblins** around the fire, then **mount a Boar** (the wild ones running around the desert) and **ram the tower** to smash it.

Requires: Boar (ride one in), combat ability to clear the camp.
