# Goron Mines Chest Before Dangoro

In the room with the **rotating magnetic planks** in Goron Mines — the corridor just before the Dangoro midboss arena.

A small chest sits on a **ledge** in that room. Iron-Boot up the rotating planks and step off onto the ledge to reach it.

Requires: **Iron Boots**.
