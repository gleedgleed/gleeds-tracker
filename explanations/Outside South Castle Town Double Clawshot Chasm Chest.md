# Outside South Castle Town Double Clawshot Chasm Chest

In the **chasm south of Castle Town** that leads down toward Lake Hylia.

Use the **Double Clawshot** to chain from one target to the next across the chasm to reach the chest.

Requires: **Double Clawshot**.
