# Lakebed Temple Morpheel

Defeat **Morpheel**, the Lakebed Temple boss (giant eel in the central pool).

**Iron Boots** down to stay on the bottom, **Clawshot the eye** on Morpheel's tongue to yank it out, then sword it. In phase 2 (full-body Morpheel swimming around), Clawshot its eye and stab.

Requires: **Iron Boots**, **Clawshot**.
