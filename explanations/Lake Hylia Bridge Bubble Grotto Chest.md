# Lake Hylia Bridge Bubble Grotto Chest

Inside the **Bubble Grotto** on the Bridge of Hylia (wolf-dig grotto, full of **Bubble** enemies).

**Kill every Bubble** to spawn the chest.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal) to enter.
