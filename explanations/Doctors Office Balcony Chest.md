# Doctors Office Balcony Chest

In the **Doctor's office** in west Castle Town.

**Show the Doctor the Invoice** (Telma Invoice) — he leaves the office. Then transform into **Wolf Link** and **climb the shelves** to push them aside, opening the route to the **outside balcony**. The chest sits on the balcony.

Requires: **Telma Invoice**, **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
