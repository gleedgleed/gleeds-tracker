# City in The Sky Central Outside Ledge Chest

In the **outside room above the central fan** in City in The Sky.

As **Wolf Link**, walk the **tightropes**, kill the **Walltulas** in the way, and reach the chest on the ledge.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
