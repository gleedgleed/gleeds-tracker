# Snowpeak Ruins East Courtyard Buried Chest

In the **East Courtyard** of Snowpeak Ruins.

The chest is **buried under the snow** -- use **Wolf Link** to dig it up.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
