# Fishing Hole Bottle

In **Hena's Fishing Hole** (Upper Zora's River area, past the rapids).

Cast the **Fishing Rod** into the **small pond by the wooden bridge** — there's a chance to hook the bottle. Keep trying.

Requires: **Fishing Rod**.
