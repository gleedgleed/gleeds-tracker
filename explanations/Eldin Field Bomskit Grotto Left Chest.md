# Eldin Field Bomskit Grotto Left Chest

Inside the **Bomskit Grotto** in Eldin Field (wolf-dig entry, Bomskits inside).

A small chest hiding in a **patch of grass** to the left side of the room — cut the grass to reveal it.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal) to enter the grotto.
