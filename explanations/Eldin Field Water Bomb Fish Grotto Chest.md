# Eldin Field Water Bomb Fish Grotto Chest

Inside the **Water Bomb Fish Grotto** in Eldin Field (the wolf-dig grotto with a pool of water and Bomb Fish swimming around).

**Swim across the pool** to the ledge on the far side; the chest sits there.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal) to enter the grotto.
