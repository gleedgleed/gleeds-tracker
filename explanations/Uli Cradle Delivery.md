# Uli Cradle Delivery

The prologue **cradle quest**. Borrow the **Hawk** (cuckoo) from Sera's shop, fly it at the **monkey** carrying the cradle (the monkey scampers around the village rooftops), and bring the cradle back to Uli at her house.

Reward is given by Uli on delivery — that fires the check.
