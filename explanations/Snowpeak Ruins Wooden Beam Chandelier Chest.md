# Snowpeak Ruins Wooden Beam Chandelier Chest

In the **upper level of the wooden beam room** of Snowpeak Ruins.

**Ball-and-Chain the chandelier** to swing it, then use it to reach the chest on a higher beam / ledge.

Requires: **Ball and Chain**.
