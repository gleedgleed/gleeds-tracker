# Faron Field Corner Grotto Right Chest

Inside the **Corner Grotto** in the northeast corner of Faron Field (wolf-dig grass-circle, Keese and Rats inside).

Small chest hidden in the **tall grass on the right side** of the room. Cut the grass to reveal it.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
