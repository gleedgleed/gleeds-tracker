# Goron Mines After Crystal Switch Room Magnet Wall Chest

In the **water/crystal-switch room** of Goron Mines.

**Hit the crystal switch** to open the gate, then equip **Iron Boots** and walk up the **magnetized wall** to reach the chest.

Requires: **Iron Boots**.
