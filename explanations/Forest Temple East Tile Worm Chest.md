# Forest Temple East Tile Worm Chest

In the room **east of the Windless Bridge** in the Forest Temple.

The floor tiles hide **Tile Worms** that pop up and attack. **Throw the Gale Boomerang** to flip them onto their backs, then sword them. With all worms dead, the chest is accessible.

Requires: **Gale Boomerang**.
