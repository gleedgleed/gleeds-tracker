# Sacred Grove Baba Serpent Grotto Chest

Inside the **Baba Serpent Grotto** — a grotto in the Lost Woods, hidden under a **rock** you have to smash.

Bomb (or Ball-and-Chain) the rock open, drop in, kill the Baba Serpents, and open the chest inside.

Requires: bombs / Ball and Chain to smash the rock.
