# Outside South Castle Town Tightrope Chest

South of Castle Town, above the fountain.

**Clawshot to the right-side pillar above the fountain**, then transform with the **Shadow Crystal** into Wolf Link and **walk across the ropes** strung between pillars to reach the chest.

Requires: **Clawshot**, **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
