# Goron Mines Main Magnet Room Bottom Chest

In the **big multi-level magnet room** in Goron Mines.

The chest sits on the **lowest level** of the room. Iron-Boot down and pop it open.

Requires: **Iron Boots** (to navigate the magnet walls/ceiling).
