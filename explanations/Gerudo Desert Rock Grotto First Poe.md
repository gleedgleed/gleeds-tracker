# Gerudo Desert Rock Grotto First Poe

Inside the **Rock Grotto** below the "lone Poe ledge" east of the Bulblin Camp (boulder-filled wolf-dig grotto).

Two Poes inside; this is the **first** of them.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
