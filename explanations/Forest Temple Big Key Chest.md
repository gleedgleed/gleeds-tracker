# Forest Temple Big Key Chest

In the **East Wing** of the Forest Temple, behind a pair of wooden gates locked open by **pinwheel mechanisms**.

**Throw the Boomerang** through both pinwheels (target them in sequence) to spin them and open the gates, then walk in and open the chest.

Requires: **Gale Boomerang**.
