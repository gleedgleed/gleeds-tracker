# Faron Mist North Chest

Small chest in the poisonous mist area of North Faron Woods, on the side near the entrance of the **Faron Mist Cave** (the small east-side cave, not the long tunnel from South Faron).

Bring the **Lantern** to keep the mist back (or use **wolf form**, which is immune).
