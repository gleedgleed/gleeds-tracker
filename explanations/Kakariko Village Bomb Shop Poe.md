# Kakariko Village Bomb Shop Poe

In the **wreckage of Barnes's Bomb Shop** in Kakariko Village — after the shop has been blown up in the main story (King Bulblin's bridge attack).

Once the shop is rubble, enter as **Wolf Link**, use Senses to spot the Poe, and fight it.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), story progression past the bomb shop destruction.
