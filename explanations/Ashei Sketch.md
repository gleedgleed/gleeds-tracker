# Ashei Sketch

After entering **Snowpeak** from Zora's Domain (the upper part of the river path), talk to **Ashei** -- she's at the foot of the mountain by a small camp.

She hands you her sketch.

Requires: Snowpeak access (Zora Armor + Iron Boots to walk up the frozen river, in vanilla).
