# Iza Helping Hand

In **Upper Zora's River**, at **Iza's Shop** (river-rapids tent).

**Iza needs the river cleared** — there are **rocks blocking the rapids**. Take her **canoe**, ride downriver, and use **Bow + Bombs (Bomb Arrows)** to blast the rocks as you pass. After the run, talk to Iza for the reward.

Requires: **Bow + Bombs** (Bomb Arrows).
