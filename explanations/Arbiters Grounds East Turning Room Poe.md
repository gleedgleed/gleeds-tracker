# Arbiters Grounds East Turning Room Poe

In the **east turntable room** of Arbiter's Grounds.

After you have the **Poe Scent**, **pull the sand-chain** to access the downstairs section, **turn the lever** to rotate, then **Clawshot up** to the upper floor with the Poe.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal) with Poe Scent, **Clawshot**.
