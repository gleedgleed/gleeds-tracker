# Outside South Castle Town Tektite Grotto Chest

Inside the **Tektite Grotto** atop the **stone pillar south of Castle Town** (the pillar near the fountain — reach the top with Clawshot then dig in as wolf).

**Kill all the Tektites** inside to reveal the chest.

Requires: **Clawshot** (or other means up the pillar), **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal) for the grotto dig.
