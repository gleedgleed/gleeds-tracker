# Flight By Fowl Second Platform Chest

At **Falbi's Flight By Fowl** minigame above Lake Hylia (climb up to his house and pay to play).

Grab the **Cucco** and glide. The chest is the **highest of the four staggered floating-platform chests** below.

Requires: 10-rupee fee, control of the gliding Cucco.
