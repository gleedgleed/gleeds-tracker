# Arbiters Grounds West Chandelier Chest

In the **West Wing** of Arbiter's Grounds. After moving the **block** there (and progressing the East Wing first), **pull the sand-chain** to **raise the chandelier** -- the chest sits beneath it.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal) for the chain.
