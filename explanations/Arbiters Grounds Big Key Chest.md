# Arbiters Grounds Big Key Chest

In Arbiter's Grounds. Navigate through the corridors **past the Poe Gate** (the gate that opens once you've burned the Poe torches with the Lantern) -- the chest is at the end.

Requires: **Lantern** (for the Poe Gate), plus general dungeon progression.
