# City in The Sky Poe Above Central Fan

In the **outside fan area where you get the Big Key** in City in The Sky.

**Tightrope across all the platforms** (Wolf Link), working **west** -- the Poe is at the far end.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
