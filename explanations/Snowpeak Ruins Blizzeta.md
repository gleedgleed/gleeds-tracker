# Snowpeak Ruins Blizzeta

Defeat **Blizzeta**, the Snowpeak Ruins boss (the giant ice queen / Yeta's possessed form).

Phase 1: Yeta hops around -- **Ball and Chain** her ice shell to break it. Phase 2: she splits into spinning ice shards on the ceiling that crash down -- **Ball and Chain** the central body when it lands.

Requires: **Ball and Chain**.
