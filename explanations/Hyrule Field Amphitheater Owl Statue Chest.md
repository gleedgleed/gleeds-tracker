# Hyrule Field Amphitheater Owl Statue Chest

In the **Amphitheater area of West Hyrule Field** (the castle-ruins overlooking the Bridge of Hylia).

Use the **Powered Dominion Rod** to move the Owl Statue and use it as a stepping stone to reach the chest's ledge.

Requires: **Powered Dominion Rod**.
