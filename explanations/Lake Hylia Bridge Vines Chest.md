# Lake Hylia Bridge Vines Chest

On the **south side of the Bridge of Hylia**, hanging vines on the underside / cliff.

**Clawshot to the vines**, then hang off the ledge and **sidle along** until you reach the chest.

Requires: **Clawshot**.
