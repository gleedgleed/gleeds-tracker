# City in The Sky Chest Behind North Fan

In the **North Wing** of City in The Sky.

After **activating the north fan**, use the **Double Clawshot** to fly across to the other side of the wing -- the chest is **behind the northern fan blades**.

Requires: **Double Clawshot**.
