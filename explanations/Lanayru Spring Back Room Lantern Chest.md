# Lanayru Spring Back Room Lantern Chest

In the **back room of Lanayru Spring** (the spring at Lake Hylia near the Lakebed Temple entrance).

**Clawshot** up to reach the back room. Inside, **light the unlit torches** with the Lantern; the chest appears.

Requires: **Clawshot**, **Lantern**.
