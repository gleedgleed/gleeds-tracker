# Faron Field Poe

Under a tree in the **central area of Faron Field** — visible at night only (lantern flame floating in the dark).

Approach as **Wolf Link**, use Senses to see the Poe, then fight it.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), night-time.
