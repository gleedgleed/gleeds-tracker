# Lakebed Temple East Lower Waterwheel Bridge Chest

In the **lower East Wing** of Lakebed Temple, on the waterwheel-bridge area.

After **opening the West Waterway** (raising the water level), return to this room. With the water raised, you can cross the bridge -- **Clawshot** to grab the target near the chest.

Requires: **Clawshot**, West Waterway opened.
