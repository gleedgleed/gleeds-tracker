# Gerudo Desert Campfire West Chest

In **Gerudo Desert**, near the **central Bulblin campfire**. A wooden **tower** blocks the chest on the west side.

**Defeat the Bulblins**, then **mount a Boar** and **ram the tower** to smash it.

Requires: Boar, combat to clear camp.
