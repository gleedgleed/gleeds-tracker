# Agitha Male Snail Reward

Take the **Male Snail** golden bug to **Agitha** in her castle (the door in the south alley off Castle Town’s south plaza — sign reads “Agitha’s Castle”).

Talk to her with the bug in your possession; she rewards you on receipt.
