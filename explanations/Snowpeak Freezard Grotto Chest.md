# Snowpeak Freezard Grotto Chest

In the **Freezard grotto** by the lone tree on Snowpeak (wolf-dig grotto).

Use the **Ball and Chain** to smash the **ice and Freezards** guarding the chest.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), **Ball and Chain**.
