# Forest Temple Central Chest Hanging From Web

In the **Lobby** of the Forest Temple — a chest dangling from a **spider web** stretched across the upper part of the room.

**Cut the web** with a ranged attack: Ball and Chain, Gale Boomerang, Bow, or Clawshot all work. The chest drops to the floor.
