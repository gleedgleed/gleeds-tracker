# Kakariko Gorge Poe

**Under a tree on the hill overlooking the bridge** in Kakariko Gorge.

Approach as **Wolf Link** at night, use Senses to spot the Poe, then fight it.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
