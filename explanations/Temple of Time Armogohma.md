# Temple of Time Armogohma

Defeat **Armogohma**, the Temple of Time boss (giant Gohma spider in the high pillar room).

Shoot the eye on her back with the **Bow** to drop her. Then pick up one of the giant **Armos statues** in the arena with the **Dominion Rod** and drop / swing it on her to finish that phase. Phase 2: she splits into baby Gohmas -- sword them down.

Requires: **Bow**, **Dominion Rod**.
