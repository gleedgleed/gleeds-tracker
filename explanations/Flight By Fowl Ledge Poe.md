# Flight By Fowl Ledge Poe

Below **Falbi's house** at Lake Hylia (the Flight By Fowl building) there's a **ledge** with a Poe on it.

Grab a Cucco from Falbi's, fly out, and land on the ledge — fight as **Wolf Link**.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), Flight By Fowl access.
