# Death Mountain Alcove Chest

On a **ledge above the path up Death Mountain** (the trail past the entry gate, before the volcano area).

Two ways up:
- **Before Goron Mines:** Use the **Clawshot** to grab onto the vines hanging from the ledge above the path.
- **After Goron Mines:** Either use the clawshot, or stand on the **Goron below the ledge** and let him launch you up.

Requires: **Clawshot** (or post-Goron-Mines access to the friendly launching Goron).
