# Gerudo Desert North Peahat Poe

**East of the Bulblin Camp** in Gerudo Desert. The Poe sits on a **high ledge**.

**Clawshot the Peahat** (the floating flying-bulb plant) circling above the ledge to swing yourself up.

Requires: **Clawshot**, **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
