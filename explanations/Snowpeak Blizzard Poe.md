# Snowpeak Blizzard Poe

In the **blizzard section** climbing up Snowpeak (the part with reduced visibility).

The Poe sits **near a rock** in the snow. Fight as **Wolf Link**.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
