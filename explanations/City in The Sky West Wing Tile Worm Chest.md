# City in The Sky West Wing Tile Worm Chest

In the **West Wing** of City in The Sky, near the door to Big Baba Tower.

A chest on a ledge guarded by a **Tile Worm** -- flip it with the Gale Boomerang, climb up.

Requires: **Gale Boomerang** (or means to stun the Tile Worm).
