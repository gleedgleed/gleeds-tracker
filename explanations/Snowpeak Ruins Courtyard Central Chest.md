# Snowpeak Ruins Courtyard Central Chest

In the **central Courtyard** of Snowpeak Ruins.

The chest is encased in ice -- **shatter it** with the **cannon** (from the cannon room) or the **Ball and Chain**.

Requires: **Ball and Chain** (or cannon access).
