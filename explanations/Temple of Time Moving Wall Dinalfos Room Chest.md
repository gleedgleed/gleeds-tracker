# Temple of Time Moving Wall Dinalfos Room Chest

In the **moving-walls room with Dinalfos** in Temple of Time.

Use the **Dominion Rod** to move a **statue onto the switch** -- disables the electrical barrier blocking the chest.

Requires: **Dominion Rod**.
