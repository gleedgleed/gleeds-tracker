# Faron Field Tree Heart Piece

Sitting in the branches of a **tree near the bridge in the center of Faron Field**.

Climb onto the bridge railing, then **jump from the railing onto the tree** to land on the branch with the heart piece. (Alternatively, knock it down with the Gale Boomerang.)
