# Death Mountain Trail Poe

On a **ledge along the trail up Death Mountain**.

Reach it by standing on the **friendly Goron below** and letting him launch you up to the ledge. Approach as **Wolf Link** and fight the Poe.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), post-Goron-Mines (so the Gorons are friendly and willing to launch).
