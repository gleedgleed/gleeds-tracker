# Lakebed Temple Underwater Maze Small Chest

Inside the **underwater maze** section of Lakebed Temple.

Navigate through (Iron Boots for direction control or Zora-swim if available) to reach the small chest tucked away in the maze.

Requires: **Iron Boots** (or other underwater control).
