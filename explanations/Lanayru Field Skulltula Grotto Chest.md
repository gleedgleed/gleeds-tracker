# Lanayru Field Skulltula Grotto Chest

Inside the **Skulltula Grotto** **north of the stone bridge** in Lanayru Field (wolf-dig grotto).

**Light all the torches** with the Lantern to make the chest appear.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal) to enter, **Lantern**.
