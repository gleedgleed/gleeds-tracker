# Ordon Ranch Grotto Lantern Chest

Inside the **grotto on Ordon Ranch** (the one filled with pots). You enter from the ranch's stable area — there's a hole in the ground only **Wolf Link** can dig open.

Once inside, **use the Lantern to light both torches** — a chest appears.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), **Lantern**.
