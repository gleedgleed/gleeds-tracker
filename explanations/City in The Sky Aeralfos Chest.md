# City in The Sky Aeralfos Chest

The chest in the **Aeralfos midboss arena** in City in The Sky, revealed once the **Aeralfos** is defeated.

Aeralfos is the winged Lizalfos -- **Clawshot his shield** to pull him down, then sword him.

Requires: **Clawshot**.
