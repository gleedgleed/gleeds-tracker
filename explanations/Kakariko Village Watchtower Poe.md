# Kakariko Village Watchtower Poe

At the **base of the wooden watchtower** at the very top (north end) of Kakariko Village.

Approach as **Wolf Link** at night to spot the Poe with Senses, then fight it.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal).
