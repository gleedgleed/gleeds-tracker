# Kakariko Gorge Double Clawshot Chest

On the **tall rock spire on the north side of Kakariko Gorge** (the same spire with a free-standing item on top).

The spire has a series of **Double Clawshot targets** running up its side. Chain-clawshot from one to the next to climb up.

Requires: **Double Clawshot**.
