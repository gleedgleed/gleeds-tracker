# Eldin Spring Underwater Chest

At **Eldin Spring** (the pool just inside Kakariko Village's south entrance).

**Bomb the boulders** that block the spring's back area, then equip the **Iron Boots** and sink to the bottom — the chest is at the deepest point.

Requires: **Bombs** (or Ball and Chain to break the rocks), **Iron Boots**.
