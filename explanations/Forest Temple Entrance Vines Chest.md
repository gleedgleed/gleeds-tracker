# Forest Temple Entrance Vines Chest

In the **entrance room** of the Forest Temple — the first room past the entry door.

A wall of **vines on the right side** has **Walltulas** (spiders) on it. Kill the spiders with the slingshot or by shaking the vines, then **climb up the vines** to reach the chest on the upper ledge.
