# Snowboard Racing Prize

Once Snowpeak Ruins is complete, **race Yeto** (or Yeta) down Snowpeak on the snowboard.

You must **beat both Yeto and Yeta** in the race to claim the prize -- talk to them at the top of the mountain to start.

Requires: Snowpeak Ruins cleared.
