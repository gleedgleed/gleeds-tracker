# Eldin Field Bomb Rock Chest

In Eldin Field, **north of the entrance to Kakariko Village**.

Follow the path — there are **rocks blocking the way**. **Bomb each set of rocks** to keep advancing along the trail until you reach the ledge above the chest, then drop down to it.

Requires: **Bombs**.
