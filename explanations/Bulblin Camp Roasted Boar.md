# Bulblin Camp Roasted Boar

In the **back of the Bulblin Camp**, there's a large **roasted boar** on a spit.

**Destroy it** (Ball and Chain, Bombs) to reveal the item hidden inside.

Requires: a way to break the boar (Ball and Chain / Bombs).
