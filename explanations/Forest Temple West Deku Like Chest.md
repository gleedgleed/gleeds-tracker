# Forest Temple West Deku Like Chest

In the **West Wing** of the Forest Temple. A **Deku Like** (green floor-mouth) is parked on top of the chest.

Lob a **Bombling** into the Deku Like's mouth (use the Gale Boomerang to grab and toss one from a Bombling plant). After it pops, the chest is accessible.

Requires: **Gale Boomerang**.
