# Kakariko Village Bell Rupee

Above the sanctuary hidden in the bell. Use Bomb Arrows/Boomerang and Bombs on the bell to make the rupee appear.

Requires: Clear Eldin Twilight, Bomb Bag, Bow or Boomerang, Shadow Crystal.
