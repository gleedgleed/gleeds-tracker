# Castle Town Goron Shop Arrow Refill

For sale at the **Castle Town Goron Shop** (the Malo Mart's Goron-staffed branch in west Castle Town).

Talk to the **Adult Goron on the upper level** to buy.

Requires: Bridge of Eldin rebuilt (Goron Springwater quest), shop open.
