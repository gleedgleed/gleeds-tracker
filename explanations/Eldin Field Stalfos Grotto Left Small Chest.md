# Eldin Field Stalfos Grotto Left Small Chest

Inside the **Stalfos Grotto** (the wolf-dig grotto **north of Hidden Village**, in Lanayru Field / Hyrule Field).

A small chest hidden in **tall grass on the left** of the room. Cut the grass.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal) to enter the grotto.
