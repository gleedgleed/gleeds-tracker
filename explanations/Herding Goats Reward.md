# Herding Goats Reward

At **Ordon Ranch** (west end of Ordon Village). Talk to **Fado**, mount **Epona**, and complete the **Goat Herding Minigame in under 2 minutes** — that's the trigger.

Faster than the regular prologue herding; you need to clear the whole pen quickly.
