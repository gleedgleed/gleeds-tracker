# Isle of Riches Poe

At the **Isle of Riches** in Lake Hylia (the second platform of the chain). Reached via Flight By Fowl Cucco glide.

Fight as **Wolf Link**.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), Flight By Fowl access.
