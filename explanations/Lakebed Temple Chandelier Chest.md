# Lakebed Temple Chandelier Chest

In the **central hub** of Lakebed Temple, hidden inside the **chandelier hanging from the ceiling**.

**Clawshot the chandelier** to drop it; the chest falls / is exposed.

Requires: **Clawshot**.
