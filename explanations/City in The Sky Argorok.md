# City in The Sky Argorok

Defeat **Argorok**, the City in The Sky boss (giant armored dragon flying above the arena).

Phase 1: **Clawshot the Peahats** to fly around the towers, **Clawshot Argorok's tail** to drag him down, then sword the gem on his back. Phase 2: same Peahat-flying + Clawshot-tail, but the towers shrink.

Requires: **Double Clawshot**.
