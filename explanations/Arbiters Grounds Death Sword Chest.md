# Arbiters Grounds Death Sword Chest

The chest that appears in the **Death Sword midboss room** of Arbiter's Grounds, after defeating **Death Sword**.

To fight Death Sword: shoot the **Poe lantern with the Bow** to expose the floating sword, then sword it down (Ending Blow recommended).

Requires: **Bow**.
