# Faron Woods Golden Wolf

The **Golden Wolf** that appears in **North Faron Woods**, in the clearing **opposite Trill's shop** (the hollow-tree birdseed shop near the Forest Temple bridge), once Faron Twilight is cleared.

Approach him **in wolf form** (Shadow Crystal) to be pulled into the Hero's Shade vision and learn the **Ending Blow** hidden skill.

Requires: **Wolf Form** (Shadow Crystal), Faron Twilight cleared.
