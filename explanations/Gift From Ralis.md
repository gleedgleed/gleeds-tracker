# Gift From Ralis

Given by **Prince Ralis** in Kakariko (he convalesces in Renado's sanctuary).

Two prerequisites: first obtain **Rutela's Blessing** (the Zora Armor reward from Queen Rutela in the graveyard), then **show Ralis the Ashei's Sketch**. He hands over the gift in response.

Requires: Rutela's Blessing acquired, **Ashei's Sketch**.
